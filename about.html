About Findwebs

Findwebs is a directory of useful websites. It organizes practical tools, resources, and online platforms into clear categories so people can find them fast — without endless searching or ad-driven lists.

The idea is simple: every website listed here is one that is genuinely useful to a broad audience. Findwebs is a discovery product, not a blog, a store, or a service with accounts. Browse, search, and visit.

How websites are chosen

  • Useful first. A website is listed when it does a real job — creating, learning, organizing, paying, finding things — rather than existing for traffic.
  • Stable and accessible. Sites should be reliable, publicly accessible, and not gated behind a forced account to be useful.
  • Hand-reviewed. Listings are added and reviewed by the Findwebs editors. "Featured" and "Trending" are editorial choices, not calculated scores.
  • Kept general-purpose. Findwebs covers many areas of life online and does not specialize in one industry.

What you can do here

  • Search the whole directory by name, tag, category, or keyword.
  • Browse every category, from entertainment and AI tools to books, productivity, design, and development.
  • Open the information page for any listed website, then visit the site itself.
